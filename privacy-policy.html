We may collect personal information in the following situations:

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies and lightweight analytics scripts to ensure seamless navigation, remember user form preferences, and measure page load speed. You may adjust your browser settings at any time to block or notify you about cookies.

All data transmitted through our website is protected using standard 256-bit SSL (HTTPS) encryption. Access to client databases and audit records is strictly restricted to authorized SEO Ranker staff.
